**Searched for but not found as public datasets:** the 3,211-trajectory corpus of Pitre et al. 2026 ([2610.32060](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32060)). No dataset link appears in the paper's text, so it is not listed.

## Conclusion for RQ-B

- RQ-B has usable public data **from day 1**: MAScope-Bench (CC-BY-4.0, failure + topology labels) and MAD/MAST-Data (CC-BY-4.0, failure labels, several frameworks). Who&When is attractive (agent-level and step-level blame) but has no declared licence.
- **All sources lack real timestamps**, and agents/messages must be parsed from raw text. Performance (time) analysis is therefore impossible on these datasets; control-flow and handover analysis are possible. This argues for adding a rebuilt or Accenture case with proper OpenTelemetry/ABM logging.

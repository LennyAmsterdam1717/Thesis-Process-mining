# Email draft to Dr. Xixi Lu (≤ 250 words)

**Subject:** Research questions – Process Mining for Agentic AI

Dear Dr. Lu,

Thank you for our meeting on 17 November. As agreed, here are my candidate research questions. They are based on a documented review of 2026 work (51 papers). One pattern stands out: apart from SAP's Agent Behavior Mining papers, almost no 2026 study applies standard process-mining techniques to LLM-agent logs. Most use sequence statistics or LLM judges.

1. **Comparing agent architectures (main candidate).** To what extent do process-mining measures (conformance to the intended process, variant structure, rework and recovery paths) distinguish ReAct, Plan-and-Execute and multi-agent architectures running the same business process, beyond success and cost? This would be a controlled experiment with ABM-style event logs and several LLMs.

2. **Multi-agent interaction.** Can organisational mining (handover-of-work, social networks) of multi-agent event logs reveal coordination patterns linked to failure and cost, and how does it compare with LLM-judge diagnosis? Public labelled traces exist, so work can start early.

3. **Conformance (fallback).** How do alignment-based and declarative conformance checking compare in detecting agent deviations, and which attributes must agent logs record?

I am checking with Accenture whether internal (non-client) agent logs are available. If they are not, I would rebuild an agent system from an Accenture scenario. All three questions work with either data source.

I would value your view on which question fits best, and I am happy to discuss.

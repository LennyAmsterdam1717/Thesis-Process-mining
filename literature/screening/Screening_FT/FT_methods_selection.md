# Selection process – full-text stage (draft)

Of the 62 reports advanced from title/abstract screening, 58 could be retrieved as full text (all via arXiv); 4 reports published only on publisher platforms could not be retrieved and are reported as not retrieved. Full-text eligibility was assessed against the same pre-specified protocol (v1.1) with full-text rules added before assessment (`ft_protocol.md`). **Unlike the title/abstract stage, full-text assessment was performed by a single AI reviewer** (Claude Opus 5.5, claude-opus-5-5, running in Claude Code), who read the text extracted from each PDF and recorded one decision, one exclusion code and the location of the deciding fact per report; there was no second independent reviewer and no adjudicator, so agreement statistics are not available for this stage. This single-reviewer design was chosen by the student to limit cost ("go light", 18 November 2026). 8 reports were excluded (7 with no treatment of agent behaviour as process or trace data, 1 single-LLM study without tool use, kept as background), and 50 studies were included before the citation check. **Second-reviewer check (added 19 November 2026).** A second, independent AI reviewer (Claude Sonnet 5.5, SR-Screener screening-reviewer agent, methodologist persona), blinded to the first decisions, re-assessed 24 reports from the extracted full text: all 8 exclusions, all 7 includes the first reviewer had rated low relevance, and a random 20% sample of the remaining includes (9 of 44; fixed seed 20261118). The two reviewers agreed on 16 of 24 reports (66.7%; Cohen's kappa 0.08; PABAK 0.33). They agreed on all 9 randomly sampled includes and on 6 of 7 low-relevance includes, but on only 1 of 8 exclusions: the second reviewer would have included 7 reports the first excluded, mostly by reading the cost/efficiency criterion (concept e) more broadly, and would have excluded 1 low-relevance include. Because the checked set was weighted towards borderline reports, these figures do not estimate agreement for the whole corpus. The second reviewer often read only part of long reports (estimated from its tool calls). All 8 disagreements were referred to the student for decision (literature/screening/fulltext_disagreements.md); no decision was changed automatically.

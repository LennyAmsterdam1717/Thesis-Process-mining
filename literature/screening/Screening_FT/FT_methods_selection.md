# Selection process – full-text stage (draft)

Of the 62 reports advanced from title/abstract screening, 58 could be retrieved as full text (all via arXiv); 4 reports published only on publisher platforms could not be retrieved and are reported as not retrieved. Full-text eligibility was assessed against the same pre-specified protocol (v1.1) with full-text rules added before assessment (`ft_protocol.md`). **Unlike the title/abstract stage, full-text assessment was performed by a single AI reviewer** (Claude Opus 5.5, claude-opus-5-5, running in Claude Code), who read the text extracted from each PDF and recorded one decision, one exclusion code and the location of the deciding fact per report; there was no second independent reviewer and no adjudicator, so agreement statistics are not available for this stage. This single-reviewer design was chosen by the student to limit cost ("go light", 18 November 2026). 8 reports were excluded (7 with no treatment of agent behaviour as process or trace data, 1 single-LLM study without tool use, kept as background), and 50 studies were included before the citation check.

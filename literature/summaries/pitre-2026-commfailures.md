# Understanding Communication Failures in Multi-Agent LLM Systems

- **Authors:** Priya Pitre; Jun Hou; Yi Fang; Naren Ramakrishnan; Xuan Wang

## What they did
Corpus-grounded taxonomy of communication failures from 3,211 failed multi-round multi-agent trajectories across six task families; analyses round-level error dynamics, recovery, majority transitions and minority-signal suppression.

## Key findings
Failures are dominated by recovery/correction and consensus problems, often emerging after agents see peer answers; minority correct answers are suppressed.

## Limitations / future work
Only failed debate-style trajectories; open-weight models; does not cover tool-using workflow agents.

## Why it matters for the thesis
Gives a failure vocabulary for multi-agent interaction that process mining (hand-over, variant analysis) could detect at scale.

# Coordination as an Architectural Layer for LLM-Based Multi-Agent Systems

- **Authors:** Maksym Nechepurenko; Pavel Shuvalov
- **Year / first version:** 2026 / 2026-05-05
- **Venue / status:** arXiv preprint – preprint
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2605.03310 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03310

- **Record:** R00037 · relevance high · citation check PASS (2026-11-18)

## What they did
Treats coordination as a configurable architectural layer; information-controlled comparison of five coordination configurations (same LLM, tools, prompts) on 100 prediction markets, with pre-specified failure-mode signatures and cost; traces released.

## Key findings
Under identical information access, coordination configurations produce distinct, partly predictable failure signatures; 3 of 5 pre-registered signatures held; traces released.

## Limitations / future work
One domain (prediction markets) and 100 markets; no formal third-party pre-registration; three of five predicted signatures held.

## Why it matters for the thesis
Released traces + controlled design: reusable public data for a direction-6 RQ.

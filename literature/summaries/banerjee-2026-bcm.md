# Measuring Cross-Task Behavioral Consistency in Language Model Agents

- **Authors:** Amritesh Banerjee; Pranil Raichura
- **Year / first version:** 2026 / 2026-07-31

## What they did
Behavioral Consistency Metric: from structural trace features of ~9,000 SWE trajectories of six agents, measures cross-task and within-task consistency and shows it is distinct from success rate.

## Key findings
Proprietary systems are highly consistent (BCM 0.77–0.83), open Llama agents much less; consistency is not reducible to success.

## Limitations / future work
Structural features only (steps, edits, errors, repetition); depends on one success predictor (AUC 0.69).

## Why it matters for the thesis
Consistency ≈ variant stability in process-mining terms; links to ABM's "variability is intrinsic" claim.

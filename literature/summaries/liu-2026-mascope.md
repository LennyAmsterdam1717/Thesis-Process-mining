# Know the Shape, Find the Fault: Topology-Conditioned Diagnosis of Multi-Agent LLM Failures

- **Authors:** Xinwen Liu; Zhuocheng Pan; Isabella Zhu; Jawei Zhang; Xudong Liu; Tianyu Wo
- **Year / first version:** 2026 / 2026-10-07
- **Venue / status:** arXiv preprint – preprint

- **Record:** R00035 · relevance high · citation check PASS (2026-11-18)

## What they did
MAScope recovers communication topology from heterogeneous multi-agent execution traces and uses it to condition failure diagnosis; releases MAScope-Bench.

## Key findings
Topology is associated with failure patterns; conditioning a judge on recovered topology improves diagnosis and lets a cheaper judge approach a stronger one.

## Limitations / future work
Empirical association between topology and failures, not causal; depends on LLM judges; limited set of frameworks.

## Why it matters for the thesis
Topology recovery from traces is close to social-network / handover-of-work mining in process mining, which nobody in 2026 applied to LLM MAS.

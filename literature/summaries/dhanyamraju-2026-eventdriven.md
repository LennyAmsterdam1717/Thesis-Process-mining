- **Authors:** Harsh Rao Dhanyamraju; Leonidas Raghav; Aaron Lee

## What they did
Controlled enterprise simulation (208 scenarios, up to 200 agents) comparing DAG Plan&Execute with ReAct orchestration on agent-call precision, recall and answer completeness, plus a Task Manager that cuts high-priority queue latency.

## Key findings
DAG Plan&Execute keeps higher agent-call precision; ReAct has better recall/completeness at enterprise scale; the Task Manager cuts high-priority queue latency by 14–75%.

## Limitations / future work
Controlled simulation, not production validation; raw enterprise material not released; results characterise the tested implementations only.

## Why it matters for the thesis
SAP enterprise setting, 200-agent registries: a strong candidate scenario to re-analyse with process mining (direction 4 + 6).

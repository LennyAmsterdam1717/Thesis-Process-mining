# When "Must" Becomes "Maybe": Erosion of Workflow Constraints in LLM Agent Handoffs

- **Authors:** Yiheng Sun; Huifei Wang; Yancheng Zhu; Zhenyu Li; Zebin Zhao; Yifan Yuan
- **Year / first version:** 2026 / 2026-08-25
- **Venue / status:** arXiv preprint – preprint
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2608.24569 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24569
- **Directions (working numbering):** 4 multi-agent interaction
- **Record:** R00030 · relevance high · citation check PASS (2026-11-18)

## What they did
Shows that handoffs between LLM agents erode upstream workflow constraints (safety blockers become caveats); measures blocker deactivation under compression, plan assimilation, ownership deferral and other handoff types.

## Key findings
Direct-state handoffs preserve every blocker; ownership deferral deactivates 76.7% and multi-hop compression 97.2% of blockers.

## Limitations / future work
Synthetic enterprise tasks with fixed permissions and finite actions; other workflow states (commitments, dependencies) not yet represented.

## Why it matters for the thesis
Hand-off content changes downstream behaviour: a conformance angle (constraint-preservation across hand-offs) for direction 4.

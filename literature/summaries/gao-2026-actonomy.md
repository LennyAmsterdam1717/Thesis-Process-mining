## What they did
Act-ONOMY: grounded-theory taxonomy of agent runtime behaviour (10 actions, 46 sub-actions, 120 leaves) with an open pipeline that labels trajectories and compares behavioural profiles across agents.

## Key findings
Act-ONOMY profiles differ across agents and expose failure-indicative patterns; open pipeline and extension protocol.

## Limitations / future work
Taxonomy built from 565 behavioural sentences in 20 papers; LLM-assisted construction; profiles may change as commercial models change.

## Why it matters for the thesis
Provides an activity vocabulary (event abstraction) that could become activity labels in agent event logs.

## What they did
AgentLTL: FO-LTL-based language for procedural rules over agent traces giving a judge-free compliance score, used for scoring, online gating and as a fine-tuning reward.

## Key findings
Block-and-warn harnessing improves compliance for five of seven models; using the compliance score as reward adds +38 / +17.5 points accuracy / compliance.

## Limitations / future work
Grounding predicate is vacuous for refusals; fine-tuning on one small base model.

## Why it matters for the thesis
Declarative (LTL) conformance for agents, close to Declare-style checking in BPM.

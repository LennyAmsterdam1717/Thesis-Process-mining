## What they did
EAIR: taxonomy of enterprise multi-agent interaction risks, executable micro-worlds with roles, policies and execution traces, and a trace-based diagnosis-and-repair pipeline for organisation-wide constraint violations.

## Key findings
Locally permitted actions by several agents can jointly break organisation-wide constraints (e.g. a shared compensation cap); trace-based diagnosis locates the boundary and guides repair.

## Limitations / future work
Micro-worlds instantiate selected risks only; one run per model and configuration in parts of the evaluation.

## Why it matters for the thesis
Business-process framing of multi-agent risk; organisation-level constraints are natural conformance targets.

# Agent trajectories as programs: fingerprinting and programming coding-agent behavior

- **Authors:** Hamidah Oderinwale
- **Year / first version:** 2026 / 2026-06-15
- **Venue / status:** arXiv preprint – preprint
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.16988 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16988

## What they did
Procedural fingerprints of ten coding agents from SWE-bench trajectories via an induced action vocabulary; an unseen trajectory is attributed to the right agent at 85.7%; similarity tracks model lineage.

## Key findings
Agents are identifiable from procedural habits (85.7% attribution); distilled student and teacher behave alike (JSD 0.25).

## Limitations / future work
Coding tasks on SWE-bench only; representation is compressive and may hide semantics.

## Why it matters for the thesis
Behavioural comparison across models (direction 6) with sequence methods; process-model-based fingerprints are an open alternative.

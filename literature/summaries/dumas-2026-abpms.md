# Agentic Business Process Management Systems

- **Authors:** Marlon Dumas; Fredrik Milani; David Chapela-Campa
- **Year / first version:** 2026 / 2026-01-25
- **Venue / status:** arXiv preprint (keynote paper, AI4BPM workshop at BPM 2025) – preprint

- **Directions (working numbering):** 7 process-aware design
- **Record:** R00060 · relevance medium · citation check PASS (2026-11-18)

## What they did
Position paper: architectural vision for Agentic BPM Systems in which process mining lets agents sense process state, reason about improvements and act, along a continuum of autonomy.

## Key findings
Positions process mining as the sensing layer of agentic BPM systems and outlines an autonomy continuum.

## Limitations / future work
Vision, no evaluation; agents are seen as users of process mining rather than as objects of it.

## Why it matters for the thesis
Agenda-setting BPM paper (Dumas et al.); helps frame why agent behaviour itself must become mineable.
